## About

A personal hobby project dedicated to the history, geography, and legacy of the Three Kingdoms period and its surrounding historical periods in ancient China.

This site primarily compiles personal maps, translations, and commentary, as well as relevant artefacts from various museums and sites.

 
## Current Features
 
- **Interactive map** — province boundaries, settlement types (provincial seats, commandery seats, county seats, military passes, landmarks), and kingdom colour coding for Wei, Shu, and Wu
- **Short Stories Collection** — translated anecdotes from classical sources, filterable by period and type
- **Titles and Positions** — 398 official titles and ranks adapted from Hong's Qing dynasty work
- **Artefacts collection** — photographed objects with period, material, dimensions, and provenance
- **Bilingual support** — English, Traditional Chinese (繁體中文), and Simplified Chinese (简体中文)
